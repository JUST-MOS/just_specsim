# JUST BGS effective-time study: work log

## Scope and frozen numerical experiment

Reference scene: 4200 m conditional sky model, extrapolated from matched ESO models at X=1 and scaled achromatically to DECam-r = 21.07 AB mag arcsec⁻²; source extinction is Qiu's observed curve within 400–790 nm and a pressure-adjusted ESO X=1 fallback outside. Reference seeing is 1.1 arcsec at 6355 Å, converted to 1.050504 arcsec at 800 nm via λ⁻⁰·²; E(B−V)=0 and photometric transmission are assumed. This is a hypothetical DESI-like reference, not a measured Lenghu sky spectrum.

Per-pixel feature weights are constructed from the disjoint 110-galaxy calibration subset, after normalizing each spectrum to BASS-r₀=19.5 and subtracting a roughly 100 Å median-filtered continuum. The fiducial is a circular exponential galaxy with half-light radius 1.5 arcsec; this differs from DESI's de Vaucouleurs fiducial and matches the source morphology assumed in the existing JUST mocks. Signal, sky Poisson, source Poisson, dark, read and correlated sky-systematic terms use the frozen simulator; total time is split into exposures no longer than 750 s.

Existing dark 1500 s and 3000 s correspond to provisional reference depths 1817.005 s and 3436.344 s; the direct nominal Redrock experiment uses **1817 s and 3436 s**, rounded down to avoid crossing the important 750 s read boundaries. Their subexposures are 750+750+317 s and 750+750+750+750+436 s. All 1332 held-out galaxies (578 of which have r₀<19.5) are included at both times; there are 2664 input scenarios. A first 8-galaxy batch is the engineering pilot and is included unchanged in the final denominator if validated.

Success is ZWARN=0, DELTACHI2>40, and |zfit−zVI|/(1+zVI)<0.0033, divided by all selected targets, with no result counted as failure. Report point estimates and one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson lower bounds; adjust over the two nominal time points per population. The time points were chosen from prior outcomes using the same galaxy population, so intervals are exploratory and do not capture the additional adaptive design or correlated reference-model uncertainties. No cross-group or cross-sky adjustment is claimed. The r₀<19.5 cut is post hoc.

For the two nominal points, the minimum success counts for a Bonferroni-adjusted 95% lower bound of at least 95% are 560/578 for r₀<19.5 and 1282/1332 for r₀<20.5. These finite-sample thresholds are not corrections for model uncertainty or adaptive selection.

## Verification and current status

High-resolution zenith sky normalized to r=21.07 with 52 guarded wavelength samples. Baseline sky+detector variance matches the frozen simulator exactly; reference depth identity agrees to <4e−10 s at tested times; nominal aperture response agrees with the depth model to <5e−16 relative. Pilot input audit reconstructed all 48 source/time/arm draws, inverse variances, masks, and resolution matrices exactly. These are engineering checks, not site calibration.

At approximately equal model depths, previous dark 3000 s and bright 3600 s yield 1288 and 1273 successes, respectively, among the same 1332 targets; the comparison is post hoc and does not validate a universal effective-depth rule. The 23 weak-line calibration spectra yield a substantially different sky-conversion estimate from the mean 110-spectrum proxy; the alternative is a deterministic sensitivity test, not an uncertainty interval.

The Redrock pilot completed: all 16/16 fits returned, with 10/16 meeting the success cut; this deliberately tiny sequential subset includes previously difficult sources and is not a population estimate. Input and output integrity checks passed. The full scan started under `caffeinate` with two Redrock workers; `nominal_run/v2/progress.json` is the restart/status record. The first 100-case production batch returned all results in 536.253 s, suggesting roughly four hours for the remaining cases if throughput stays similar. The catalog ordering is not randomized, so interim completed-batch fractions must not be reported as population estimates. The original and derived inputs are retained.

To reduce wall time, `run_nominal_prefetch.py` briefly computed disjoint batches in reverse order in `nominal_run/prefetch_2worker` while the original driver continued in ascending order. It used the identical simulator, frozen inputs, seed rule and two Redrock worker processes for each batch. One tail batch (start 1308, 24 targets, 48 fits) completed, passed the original validators, and was hard-linked to `nominal_run/v2`; the main driver validates it again and remains the sole writer of its progress ledger. During concurrent running, macOS reported a substantial increase in the cumulative swap-out counter. The optional prefetch was therefore interrupted after the first batch; the main run continues with two workers. Batch 1258's interrupted Redrock output remains **only** in staging, while its complete, validated simulated spectra and sidecars were hard-linked for later use by the main driver; Redrock will fit them afresh. Staging paths remain available because fit metadata records those paths. See `nominal_run/prefetch_2worker/stop_record.json` and `reused_input.json`. This was operational parallelism, not a change to the statistical experiment.

The small-EBV/read-boundary sensitivity and a separate ideal covariance-weighted sensitivity are documented in `split_boundary_sensitivity.json` and `weighted_coadd_sensitivity.json`. The latter reproduces equal-duration sum-coadd Q and explicit matrix inversions to numerical precision, and is continuous across the checked read boundaries. It changes the nominal Q at fractional splits and is not the model used for the Redrock spectra, so it is not a validated replacement.

Further audit found that the first-equal-Q inverse of the frozen sum-coadd model is not an effective-time identity across all nominal reference times: a nominal 3000.001 s coadd maps back to about 2789 s because of the new read. Therefore `effective_model.py` is restricted to fixed-scene comparisons of the frozen mocks, not a DESI-like online accumulator. `weighted_effective_model.py` implements a continuous, monotone ideal inverse-covariance candidate for the same stationary scenes; `audit_weighted_model.py` verifies nominal identity around the boundaries and the equal-exposure and direct covariance identities. The candidate has not been Redrock-validated. It can proxy-map each direct nominal SUM-coadd anchor to a weighted reference and weighted actual scenes at equal Q, and `analyze_weighted_thresholds.py` will report only the anchors whose completed Redrock results meet the specified criterion.

The same analysis also exports `indirect_weighted_thresholds.csv` from the pre-existing direct dark-sky Redrock anchors. If the new nominal tests do not confirm a given threshold, this prior-dark proxy can still be cited as an indirect estimate but must not be represented as a directly confirmed nominal or bright-sky success rate.

The weighted-threshold and final report generators were smoke-tested in an isolated temporary directory with synthetic placeholder counts, including the no-passing-time branch. No synthetic counts were written into this research directory; actual summaries remain pending the complete scan and independent audit.

The complete r₀<19.5 test roster was independently gathered after main batch 858. At standard reference conditions, SUM-coadd Redrock produced 555/578 = 96.02% at 1817 s (two-time-adjusted one-sided lower 94.09%) and 563/578 = 97.40% at 3436 s (lower 95.76%). Both times returned 578/578 fits. Thus the point estimate crosses at the first of the two tested times and the adjusted lower bound crosses at the second. These times were chosen using earlier outcomes from the same galaxies and are not a blind confirmatory experiment. See `nominal_bgs_complete_early.csv/json`; at that earlier checkpoint the full r₀<20.5 run was still in progress.

The full nominal scan completed and returned 2664/2664 fits. For r₀<20.5, 1817 s gives 1249/1332 = 93.77%, adjusted lower 92.33%; 3436 s gives 1289/1332 = 96.77%, adjusted lower 95.68%. `audit_nominal_results.py` independently recomputed every quality/truth outcome and binomial interval; maximum lower-bound difference was 1.03e−13. For the same 1332 targets, nominal 3436 s versus existing bright 3600 s produced 1289 versus 1273 successes, with 19 nominal-only and 3 bright-only; this post-hoc comparison does not establish the cause, but the bright result remains unconfirmed against the stricter lower-bound threshold. The weighted candidate's equal-Q scale maps the 3436 s nominal SUM-coadd anchor to 3409.63 s nominal, 2999.71 s dark and 3557.84 s bright; these are model conversions, not directly tested weighted-coadd redshift-success results.

`PRELIMINARY_zh.md` collects the existing-scene indirect estimate while the direct reference-condition scan remains in progress; the status ledger, not this timestamped paragraph, is authoritative for the count.

`smooth_atmosphere_sensitivity.py` compares the earlier Qiu-constrained smooth source-transmission candidates to the frozen segmented atmosphere while holding sky, masks and instrument fixed. The nominal X=1 candidate adopts optical-depth-linear airmass scaling from the candidate's X=1.2 spectrum. This is a proxy-only sensitivity, not a regenerated mock or a Redrock success prediction.
